❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Are these AI prompts really free to use?

Yes. Every prompt on PromptSpark Hub is free to copy and use — including in commercial projects and client work. The AI tools you run them in (Veo 3, Midjourney, ChatGPT, etc.) have their own free tiers and paid plans, so check each tool's pricing for generation limits.

How do I copy a prompt into the AI tool?

Hit the Copy button on any prompt card — the full prompt text lands in your clipboard instantly. Open your AI tool, paste it into the prompt box, keep the aspect-ratio and negative-prompt details, and generate. The how-to steps under each prompt walk you through the tool-specific settings.

I'm a beginner — where should I start?

Filter by the Beginner difficulty badge. Beginner prompts need nothing beyond pasting the text — like the cinematic drone flyover for Veo 3 or the viral hooks writer for ChatGPT. Each one includes numbered steps, so you can't get lost.

Can I use the same prompt on a different AI tool?

Often, yes — the principles transfer. But each tool has quirks: Kling loves explicit loop instructions, Sora handles longer continuous motion, and Google Flow rewards multi-scene breakdowns. If a prompt was written for one tool, try it on yours and adjust the motion verbs if the output drifts.

How often are new prompts added?

Daily. The library grows every day with fresh prompts across video, image and text categories and all nine covered AI tools — check back or just re-run your favorite search to find what's new.

Do I need to disclose that my content is AI-generated?

It depends on the platform and the content type — there is no single rule that covers everything. As a guide: realistic AI content that a viewer could mistake for real (a real person saying something they didn't say, a realistic scene that never happened, AI music as the main focus) generally must be disclosed, while clearly unrealistic content and minor production help (scripts, captions, color tweaks) generally do not. Platforms change these rules, so check the current official policy before you publish: YouTube's “Disclosing use of GenAI content” help page, TikTok's AI-generated content help page, and Meta's AI labeling guidance for Facebook and Instagram. When in doubt, label it. No credit to PromptSpark Hub is required.
