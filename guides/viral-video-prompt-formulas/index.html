🎬 10 Viral Video Prompt Formulas for TikTok and Reels

Copyable AI video prompt templates for Veo 3, Kling, Sora & Runway — built from patterns behind millions of views.

Viral short-form videos aren't random. The same visual patterns repeat across millions of views: impossible scale, satisfying transformations, tiny worlds, dramatic reveals. AI video tools can generate all of them — if your prompt follows the right formula.

Below are 10 formulas distilled from high-performing AI video content. Each includes a copyable template: replace the bracketed parts with your own ideas.

1. The Tiny World

Why it works: miniature scenes trigger curiosity and delight — viewers can't look away.

Template — works on Veo 3, Kling
Macro photograph of a tiny [WORKER/CHARACTER] [ACTION] inside/on a [GIANT EVERYDAY OBJECT], [SETTING] background softly blurred, warm lighting, extreme detail, shallow depth of field, whimsical and charming

Example: tiny chef cooking inside a giant frying pan, kitchen background blurred. Find tiny-world prompts →

2. The Impossible Scale

Why it works: giant objects in normal settings break expectations instantly.

Template — works on Veo 3, Sora
Cinematic wide shot, a gigantic [OBJECT] [LOCATION], tiny [PEOPLE/VEHICLES] nearby for scale, dramatic [LIGHTING/WEATHER], photorealistic, epic atmosphere, slow camera push-in

Example: a gigantic strawberry the size of a house in a suburban street, tiny cars nearby, golden-hour light.

3. The Satisfying Transformation

Why it works: before-to-after reveals trigger completion satisfaction — huge watch-through rates.

Template — works on Runway, Kling
Split-screen time-lapse: left side shows [MESSY/BEFORE STATE], right side shows [BEAUTIFUL/AFTER STATE], smooth morphing transition in the center, [SETTING], satisfying and clean, upbeat energy

Example: messy desk transforming into a perfect workspace setup.

4. The POV Adventure

Why it works: first-person perspective creates immersion — viewers feel they're inside the video.

Template — works on Sora, Veo 3
First-person POV shot, [MOVING THROUGH/DOING] [EXOTIC LOCATION], hands occasionally visible, natural head-bob motion, [TIME OF DAY] lighting, immersive and realistic, continuous smooth movement

Example: POV walking through a glowing crystal cave, lantern in hand, bioluminescent light.

5. The Loop

Why it works: seamless loops get rewatched automatically — the algorithm loves replays.

Template — works on Kling, Runway
Seamless infinite loop of [CYCLICAL ACTION], [SUBJECT] in [SETTING], smooth continuous motion where the end matches the beginning perfectly, hypnotic and satisfying

Example: endless waterfall of glowing liquid chocolate looping perfectly.

6. The Food Spectacle

Why it works: ultra-detailed food content is one of the most reliable viral categories ever.

Template — works on Veo 3, Kling
Extreme close-up slow motion of [FOOD] [ACTION: being sliced/poured/stretched], ultra-detailed textures, [BACKGROUND] blurred, professional food cinematography, mouth-watering lighting

Example: molten cheese stretching in extreme slow motion, dark rustic background.

7. The Creature Reveal

Why it works: cute or astonishing creatures trigger shares — people send these to friends.

Template — works on Veo 3, Sora
Adorable [FANTASY/REAL CREATURE] [DOING SOMETHING UNEXPECTED] in [SETTING], big expressive eyes, soft fur/feathers detail, gentle camera movement, heartwarming, Pixar-like charm

Example: a tiny dragon baking cookies in a cozy cottage kitchen.

8. The Drone Flyover

Why it works: sweeping aerial shots of stunning places are endlessly watchable.

Template — works on Sora, Veo 3
Smooth drone flyover of [STUNNING LOCATION] at [GOLDEN HOUR/SUNRISE], camera gliding forward and slightly descending, [NATURAL FEATURES] below, cinematic color grade, epic and serene

Example: drone gliding over floating islands with waterfalls at sunrise.

9. The ASMR Texture

Why it works: tactile close-ups trigger sensory satisfaction even without sound.

Template — works on Kling, Veo 3
Macro shot of [TEXTURED MATERIAL] being [PRESSED/CUT/SHAPED] in slow motion, extreme surface detail, soft studio lighting, deeply satisfying, no human hands visible

Example: kinetic sand being sliced in macro slow motion.

10. The Style Mashup

Why it works: familiar things in unexpected art styles create instant novelty.

Template — works on Kling, Midjourney (image)
[FAMILIAR SUBJECT] reimagined in [UNEXPECTED ART STYLE], [SETTING], bold stylized details, vibrant colors, artistic and eye-catching, trending digital art aesthetic

Example: a New York City street reimagined as a Studio Ghibli anime scene.

How to Use These Formulas

  1. Pick one formula that fits your niche — don't mix formulas in one video.
  2. Fill the brackets with specific, vivid choices. Specificity is everything.
  3. Add camera language: "slow push-in," "top-down," "tracking shot" — camera words dramatically improve video prompts.
  4. Generate 3–5 variations and pick the best. Never post the first generation.
  5. Post consistently — one formula, many variations, daily for 30 days. That's how accounts grow.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI video tool is best for these formulas?
Veo 3 and Kling handle most formulas well. Sora excels at POV and flyovers (long coherent motion). Runway is strong for transformations and commercial looks. Try the free tiers and see which matches your style.

How long should the video be?
For TikTok/Reels: 5–15 seconds is the sweet spot for these formulas. Loops can be shorter (3–7s). Generate at the tool's default, then trim.

Can I use these commercially?
The formulas are free to use. Check your AI tool's terms for commercial rights on generated videos — most paid tiers allow it.

Why aren't my videos getting views?
Common causes: generic prompts (not specific enough), posting inconsistently, weak hooks in the first second, or no captions/hashtags. The formula gets you the visual — packaging gets you the views.
