10 Viral Video Prompt Formulas for TikTok and Reels

By PromptSpark Hub · Updated October 2026

Viral short-form videos are not random. The same visual patterns repeat across millions of views: impossible scale, satisfying transformations, tiny worlds, dramatic reveals. AI video tools can generate all of them — if your prompt follows the right formula.

Below are 10 formulas distilled from high-performing AI video content. Each includes a copyable template: replace the bracketed parts with your own ideas.

1. The Tiny World

Why it works: miniature scenes trigger curiosity and delight — viewers cannot look away.

Template (Veo 3, Kling): 'Macro photograph of a tiny [WORKER/CHARACTER] [ACTION] inside/on a [GIANT EVERYDAY OBJECT], [SETTING] background softly blurred, warm lighting, extreme detail, shallow depth of field, whimsical and charming'

Example: tiny chef cooking inside a giant frying pan, kitchen background blurred. Try our tiny-worlds macro timelapse prompt for Veo 3 to see this formula in action.

2. The Impossible Scale

Why it works: giant objects in normal settings break expectations instantly.

Template (Veo 3, Sora): 'Cinematic wide shot, a gigantic [OBJECT] [LOCATION], tiny [PEOPLE/VEHICLES] nearby for scale, dramatic [LIGHTING/WEATHER], photorealistic, epic atmosphere, slow camera push-in'

Example: a gigantic strawberry the size of a house in a suburban street, tiny cars nearby, golden-hour light.

3. The Satisfying Transformation

Why it works: before-to-after reveals trigger completion satisfaction — huge watch-through rates.

Template (Runway, Kling): 'Split-screen time-lapse: left side shows [MESSY/BEFORE STATE], right side shows [BEAUTIFUL/AFTER STATE], smooth morphing transition in the center, [SETTING], satisfying and clean, upbeat energy'

Example: messy desk transforming into a perfect workspace setup. See our before-after transformation prompt for a working template.

4. The POV Adventure

Why it works: first-person perspective creates immersion — viewers feel they are inside the video.

Template (Sora, Veo 3): 'First-person POV shot, [MOVING THROUGH/DOING] [EXOTIC LOCATION], hands occasionally visible, natural head-bob motion, [TIME OF DAY] lighting, immersive and realistic, continuous smooth movement'

Example: POV walking through a glowing crystal cave, lantern in hand. Our POV walking tour prompt for Sora nails this formula.

5. The Loop

Why it works: seamless loops get rewatched automatically — the algorithm loves replays.

Template (Kling, Runway): 'Seamless infinite loop of [CYCLICAL ACTION], [SUBJECT] in [SETTING], smooth continuous motion where the end matches the beginning perfectly, hypnotic and satisfying'

Example: endless waterfall of glowing liquid chocolate looping perfectly. Study the satisfying paint-pour loop for Kling for the loop technique.

6. The Food Spectacle

Why it works: ultra-detailed food content is one of the most reliable viral categories ever.

Template (Veo 3, Kling): 'Extreme close-up slow motion of [FOOD] [ACTION: being sliced/poured/stretched], ultra-detailed textures, [BACKGROUND] blurred, professional food cinematography, mouth-watering lighting'

Example: molten cheese stretching in extreme slow motion, dark rustic background. Our street-food prompt for Runway shows the food formula at work.

7. The Creature Reveal

Why it works: cute or astonishing creatures trigger shares — people send these to friends.

Template (Veo 3, Sora): 'Adorable [FANTASY/REAL CREATURE] [DOING SOMETHING UNEXPECTED] in [SETTING], big expressive eyes, soft fur/feathers detail, gentle camera movement, heartwarming, Pixar-like charm'

Example: a tiny dragon baking cookies in a cozy cottage kitchen. Try our POV animal adventure prompt for Kling.

8. The Drone Flyover

Why it works: sweeping aerial shots of stunning places are endlessly watchable.

Template (Sora, Veo 3): 'Smooth drone flyover of [STUNNING LOCATION] at [GOLDEN HOUR/SUNRISE], camera gliding forward and slightly descending, [NATURAL FEATURES] below, cinematic color grade, epic and serene'

Example: drone gliding over floating islands with waterfalls at sunrise. The cinematic drone flyover for Veo 3 is the definitive template.

9. The ASMR Texture

Why it works: tactile close-ups trigger sensory satisfaction even without sound.

Template (Kling, Veo 3): 'Macro shot of [TEXTURED MATERIAL] being [PRESSED/CUT/SHAPED] in slow motion, extreme surface detail, soft studio lighting, deeply satisfying, no human hands visible'

Example: kinetic sand being sliced in macro slow motion. Our ASMR cutting prompt for Veo 3 demonstrates the texture formula.

10. The Style Mashup

Why it works: familiar things in unexpected art styles create instant novelty.

Template (Kling, Midjourney for images): '[FAMILIAR SUBJECT] reimagined in [UNEXPECTED ART STYLE], [SETTING], bold stylized details, vibrant colors, artistic and eye-catching, trending digital art aesthetic'

Example: a New York City street reimagined as a Studio Ghibli anime scene. See our anime scene for Kling.

How to Use These Formulas

Pick one formula that fits your niche — do not mix formulas in one video. Fill the brackets with specific, vivid choices. Specificity is everything. Add camera language: 'slow push-in,' 'top-down,' 'tracking shot' — camera words dramatically improve video prompts. Generate 3-5 variations and pick the best. Never post the first generation. Post consistently — one formula, many variations, daily for 30 days. That is how accounts grow.

These 10 formulas cover the visual patterns behind most viral AI short-form content. Master one before moving to the next — depth beats breadth. Pair any formula with solid camera language and lighting direction (see the drone flyover prompt for a textbook example), generate 3-5 variations per idea, and post daily. Consistency with a proven formula beats random creativity every time.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which AI video tool is best for these formulas?
Veo 3 and Kling handle most formulas well. Sora excels at POV and flyovers (long coherent motion). Runway is strong for transformations and commercial looks. Try the free tiers and see which matches your style.

How long should the video be?
For TikTok/Reels: 5-15 seconds is the sweet spot for these formulas. Loops can be shorter (3-7s). Generate at the tool's default, then trim.

Can I use these commercially?
The formulas are free to use. Check your AI tool's terms for commercial rights on generated videos — most paid tiers allow it.

Why are not my videos getting views?
Common causes: generic prompts (not specific enough), posting inconsistently, weak hooks in the first second, or no captions/hashtags. The formula gets you the visual — packaging gets you the views.
