Cinematic geopolitical documentary scene, high-stakes and epic, [GEOPOLITICS STORY: a massive aircraft carrier at golden sunset with fighter jets on deck, ocean spray and dramatic sky / a hypersonic missile streaking through an orange sky leaving a bright vapour trail over mountains / a stealth submarine surfacing through dark arctic ice at dawn in cold blue mist / a swarm of military drones flying in formation over a futuristic city skyline at dusk], movie-trailer lighting, serious and awe-inspiring, 16:9, photorealistic

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Generate one power story in Veo 3 per video, swapping only [GEOPOLITICS STORY] — one weapon, one fleet or one standoff per video; never explain three wars in one clip.
  2. Script each video in ChatGPT as a question + answer arc: 'Why does one aircraft carrier change a whole region?' — a question title promises an answer viewers must stay for.
  3. Voice it in ElevenLabs with a deep, calm documentary narrator tone — geopolitics lives on authority; hype makes serious power sound like a game.
  4. Use maps, numbers and comparisons on screen (range, speed, cost) — concrete numbers are what viewers screenshot, quote and argue about.
  5. Post 2x/week (Monday + Thursday) as a series — 'Power 03: The Submarine Nobody Can Find' — numbered power stories turn casual viewers into subscribers.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: open on the most shocking capability first ('This missile reaches its target in 6 minutes') — capability numbers stop the scroll faster than country names.
  • Title trick: use the question formula 'Why [Country] Built [Weapon] — And Why Everyone Is Worried' — the worry in the title promises stakes, not just facts.
  • Comment bait: end with 'Deterrent or danger? Comment below' — verdict questions split the audience and double comments.
