Luxury POV Travel Walkthrough — Sora Prompt for Instagram Theme Pages

First-person walkthrough of a jaw-dropping luxury resort suite and infinity pool at sunset — the aspirational travel aesthetic that grows Instagram theme pages fast.

AI ToolSora
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

Immersive first-person walkthrough of a breathtaking luxury resort: starting in a marble lobby with a cascading chandelier, stepping onto a private balcony overlooking a turquoise ocean, then a slow glide along an infinity pool at golden sunset with palm silhouettes, steam rising off the warm water, a server walking past with champagne, warm cinematic color grade, smooth gimbal-like camera motion, distant waves and ambient resort sounds implied, vertical 9:16, photorealistic, ultra detailed, no text, no watermark, no people in close-up

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Sora and create a new video generation.
  2. Paste the prompt and keep the three-location sequence — the lobby-to-pool journey is the hook.
  3. Set duration to the maximum available so the walkthrough feels like one continuous glide.
  4. Generate twice and choose the clip with the smoothest camera transitions between scenes.
  5. Layer trending travel audio over the clip and pin a comment naming the 'resort' to drive comment bait.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Never claim the location is real — 'POV: you saved 10 years for this' captions keep it fantasy and safe.
  • One niche per page: separate accounts for beach resorts, city suites and mountain lodges grow faster than mixed feeds.
