Rising Subculture Niche Finder — ChatGPT Prompt to Spot Creator Niches Before They Explode

Find fast-rising subcultures and turn them into first-mover channels — a niche-discovery prompt that scores trend velocity, competition and monetization before you commit.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a trend-spotting strategist for faceless content creators. Step 1: list 15 fast-rising subcultures, hobbies or internet tribes in 2026, each with 2-3 pieces of evidence (search growth, viral posts, new communities, product launches). Step 2: for each subculture, score it 1-10 on trend velocity, audience size, competition level, content supply difficulty and monetization potential, and explain each score in one line. Step 3: rank the top 5 and, for each, give 3 channel angles that have NOT been done to death, 10 video ideas with hooks, the best platform (YouTube, TikTok, Facebook or Instagram) and why, and the first 30-day posting plan. Step 4: flag the 3 riskiest picks and what would kill them. Rules: no generic niches (fitness, cooking, finance), every claim must cite a signal, and the final output must be a decision table I can act on today.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Paste the prompt into ChatGPT and add your constraints at the end (faceless or on-camera, your weekly hours, your budget) — constraints sharpen the shortlist dramatically.
  2. Run it once per month, not daily — subcultures move monthly, and re-running weekly just reshuffles noise into false urgency.
  3. Take the top 3 ranked niches and validate each with one week of real research (search the platform, count serious competitors, check monetization) before committing.
  4. Pick ONE niche and ONE angle from the output — the prompt's job is to narrow, and creators fail by launching three niches at once.
  5. Re-run with 'exclude everything from the last run' to force fresh picks each month and build a pipeline of backup niches.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Evidence rule: reject any subculture whose evidence is only vibes — demand a number, a post, or a community for every pick.
  • First-mover rule: the best pick is usually rank 2-3, not rank 1 — rank 1 is where everyone else is already rushing.
  • Kill-switch rule: write down what would make you quit the niche BEFORE launching — it stops sunk-cost months later.
