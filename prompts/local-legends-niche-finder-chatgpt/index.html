Local Legends Niche Finder — ChatGPT Prompt to Turn Hometown Stories Into Viral Channels

Mine the haunted houses, lost treasures and strange characters of your own town for a channel nobody else on Earth can copy — local stories with global curiosity.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyBeginner

📋 Copy the Prompt

Act as a viral niche researcher for faceless YouTube and TikTok channels. I live in [YOUR TOWN OR REGION]. Research-style brainstorm: list 20 local legends, mysteries, forgotten places and colourful characters from my area that could each become a video series, including haunted buildings, unsolved events, lost treasures, strange local traditions and eccentric historical figures. For each idea give: a curiosity-driven video title, a one-line hook, why viewers outside my town would care, and whether it suits YouTube long-form, TikTok Shorts or Facebook Reels. Then rank the top 5 by uniqueness, story depth and how hard they would be for another creator to copy. Finish with a 30-day posting plan for the number one niche, with one video idea per day.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT and paste the prompt, replacing [YOUR TOWN OR REGION] with your real town, city or region.
  2. Read the ranked top 5 first and pick the niche with the strongest stories, not just the strangest title.
  3. Ask ChatGPT to expand the winning niche into 10 full video scripts with hooks, so you launch with a backlog, not one video.
  4. Verify every legend against two local sources before filming — local viewers will correct errors fast and publicly.
  5. Post the first video with your town's name in the title; locals share it first, and the algorithm carries it outward.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Your unfair advantage is access: film the real street, the real building, the real archive — stock footage cannot compete.
  • Interview one older local per month; their voice makes the channel uncopyable and deeply trusted.
  • Never invent a legend wholesale — embellish the telling, never the facts, or local trust collapses.
