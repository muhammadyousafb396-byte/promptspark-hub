Trend Early-Warning System — Gemini Prompt to Catch Niches Before They Peak

Gemini builds you a weekly early-warning routine for rising trends in your niche — where to look, what signals matter, and how to post while a trend is still cheap to ride.

AI ToolGemini
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a trend-forecasting analyst for content creators. My niche: [NICHE]. My main platform: [PLATFORM]. Build me a TREND EARLY-WARNING SYSTEM I can run in 20 minutes every Monday. PART 1 — SIGNAL SOURCES: the 8 specific places (search bars, comment sections, adjacent niches, other platforms, seasonal calendars) where trends in my niche appear FIRST, and what exactly to look for in each. PART 2 — SIGNAL SCORING: a simple 1-5 scoring rubric for any trend I spot (search growth, creator count, cross-platform spread, monetization fit) with the minimum score worth acting on. PART 3 — THE 48-HOUR RULE: how to turn a spotted trend into a post within 48 hours — the fast format, the hook formula, and what to skip when speed matters. PART 4 — FALSE ALARMS: 5 signs a 'trend' is actually a one-video fluke or a saturated wave I am already too late for. PART 5 — give me a printable one-page weekly checklist. End with 3 trend predictions for my niche for the next 60 days and the reasoning behind each.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Gemini and paste the prompt with your niche and platform filled in.
  2. Print or save the Part 5 checklist — the system only works if you actually run it weekly.
  3. Score every trend you spot with the Part 2 rubric before filming anything.
  4. When a trend passes the score, use the Part 3 48-hour playbook instead of planning a 'perfect' video.
  5. Re-run the predictions section monthly and grade Gemini's old predictions — it sharpens your own trend instincts.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Trends usually jump platforms (TikTok first, Reels and Shorts 2-3 weeks later) — spotting the jump is the cheapest growth hack in 2026.
  • Keep a simple trend log: date spotted, score, posted or skipped, result. After 8 weeks the log tells you which signals YOUR audience rewards.
