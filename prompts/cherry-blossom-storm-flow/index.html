Cherry Blossom Storm — Google Flow Prompt for Viral Facebook Reels Niches

A Japanese temple garden caught in a swirling storm of cherry blossom petals — the soft, shareable beauty loop that Facebook's nature-aesthetic pages can't stop reposting.

AI ToolGoogle Flow
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyBeginner

📋 Copy the Prompt

Generate a 10-second 9:16 vertical loop in Google Flow: a serene Japanese temple garden at golden hour — a vermilion pagoda, a koi pond, a stone lantern path. TIMELINE: (0-2) a gentle breeze stirs, petals beginning to lift from ancient cherry trees (2-5) the storm peaks — thousands of pink petals swirling in a slow vortex around the pagoda, koi darting beneath the surface (5-8) petals settling onto the pond like pink snow, ripples spreading (8-10) one petal lands on the stone lantern in the foreground — the exact frame we opened on. Loop-perfect: opening and closing frames must match for an invisible repeat. Style: ultra-realistic cinematic nature, warm golden light through pink petals, glassy water reflections, delicate depth of field. Implied sound: soft wind, petal rustle, distant temple bell, water trickle. NO people, NO text, NO watermark — pure tranquility.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Google Flow, 9:16, 10 seconds, paste the prompt — verify the loop point by comparing frame 0 and frame 10 side by side; the petal on the lantern must sit identically.
  2. Loop 3x for a 30-second Reel; Facebook's serenity audience watches these on repeat, and loops farm watch-time silently.
  3. Overlay a soft piano or koto track — aesthetic pages get saved and shared on music fit alone.
  4. Caption: 'Send this to someone who needs 30 seconds of calm' — DM-able content spreads faster than comment-bait on Facebook.
  5. Post Sunday mornings; calm content overperforms on weekends when people are doomscrolling for relief.
  6. Seasonal twins: autumn maple storm, winter snow over the same garden — one location, four viral seasons.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The petal vortex (seconds 2-5) is the hook — if it renders as a light sprinkle, add 'dense, dramatic petal storm' and re-render.
  • The koi darting under petals is a bonus scroll-stopper: visible orange flashes under pink water double the detail viewers notice on rewatch.
  • Keep the pagoda slightly off-center — centered landmarks look like postcards; off-center looks like cinema.
