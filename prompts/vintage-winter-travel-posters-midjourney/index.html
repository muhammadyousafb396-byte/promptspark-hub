Vintage Winter Travel Poster Pack — Midjourney Prompt for Instagram & Facebook Art Pages

A set of vintage travel posters for winter places that never existed — a ski lodge on the moon, a train to the North Pole, a beach resort inside an iceberg. Retro poster art is Instagram's most saved image format, and imaginary winter destinations are a theme page angle nobody has packed yet.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Midjourney image, [ASPECT: 4:5]: a vintage 1950s travel poster for an imaginary winter destination — a ski lodge on the moon with tiny skiers under Earthrise, bold retro typography space left blank at the bottom, flat colours, screen-print texture, warm red and cream palette, nostalgic and charming, highly detailed, --ar 4:5 --v 6 --style raw

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Midjourney and paste the full prompt above.
  2. Swap only the destination in each run — moon ski lodge, North Pole train, iceberg beach resort — and keep the palette and poster style identical so the pack feels like one series.
  3. Generate 4 versions per destination and keep the one with the cleanest empty space at the bottom for your own text.
  4. Post one poster per day as a carousel, and ask followers which imaginary destination they would book first.
  5. Add your own short destination name in the blank space with a retro font in your editor — the text makes each poster shareable.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The blank space at the bottom is the feature — if Midjourney fills it with gibberish text, add '--no text, letters' to the prompt.
  • Keep the palette to two or three colours per poster; limited palettes look more vintage and more premium.
  • Sell the pack as a printable set after posting — poster packs are the easiest first product for an art page.
