Glass Fruit Cutting ASMR — Crystal-Clear Slicing Satisfying Reels (Facebook Viral Niche)

Two cravings collide — food and the impossible: glass you can slice triggers a curiosity gap ('how does that even sound?'), and the crisp slice sound delivers the ASMR payoff that keeps viewers replaying. Is niche ki videos 187.3M/142.8M/118.5M/86.2M views tak gayi hain.

📋 Copy the Prompt

VIDEO PROMPT (Runway / Kling / Veo 3) — paste as-is:

"Create a vertical 15-second ultra-macro glass fruit ASMR clip. A razor-sharp chef's knife slowly slices a translucent crystal-clear glass [FRUIT: watermelon / strawberry / orange / pineapple] on a dark reflective cutting board. Sparkling crystal shards scatter in slow motion, rainbow light refractions dance through the glass fruit, crisp slicing sounds, hyper-detailed, glossy, vibrant colors, satisfying loop ending — no text inside the video."

[VARIABLE] slots to remix: [FRUIT]

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Step 1 — Generate 4 clips, one per [FRUIT], with the exact same dark board + lighting words in every prompt, so the series looks like one account's signature style.
  2. Step 2 — Edit: knife touches the glass fruit in the FIRST second, slow-mo slice through the middle, end on the sparkling cross-section held for 1 second — loop-friendly; keep the real slicing sound loud.
  3. Step 3 — Post as a Facebook Reel, caption asks a question ('Which fruit should be glass next? 🍉'), and reshare to your Page story in the same hour for the distribution boost.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule (Facebook): knife must TOUCH the glass fruit within the first second — the scrape sound is the hook; never open with a static fruit shot.
  • Caption trick: 'Wait for the sound 🔊' + a question — Facebook pushes Reels with active comment threads, and sound-on viewers retain longer.
  • Post 6–9pm US; repost the best performer as a Story the next morning — Facebook counts Story reshares as a distribution signal.
