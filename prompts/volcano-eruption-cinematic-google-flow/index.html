Volcano Eruption Cinematic — Google Flow Prompt for Viral Shorts

📋 Copy the Prompt

Epic cinematic volcano eruption at dusk, a massive plume of ash rising into a purple-orange sky, rivers of glowing molten lava flowing down dark rocky slopes, lightning crackling inside the ash cloud, slow-motion embers and sparks drifting toward the camera, dramatic low-angle drone shot sweeping over the lava flows, intense orange glow against deep blue twilight, photorealistic, hyper-detailed, 9:16 vertical, no text, no watermark, no people

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Google Flow and start a new video generation.
  2. Paste the full prompt with all atmosphere and lighting details.
  3. Choose 9:16 vertical for Shorts and TikTok.
  4. Generate 2-3 takes — keep the one with the most dramatic ash cloud.
  5. Add a shocking fact caption and disaster-niche hashtags before posting.

💡 Pro Tips

  • First frame must show lava — thumbnails with fire get the highest CTR.
  • Follow with an 'eruption aftermath' clip for a viral two-part series.
