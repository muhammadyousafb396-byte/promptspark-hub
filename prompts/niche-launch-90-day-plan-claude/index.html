90-Day Niche Launch Plan — Claude Prompt from Idea to First 1,000 Followers

Claude builds your complete 90-day launch plan for a new niche page — content calendar, posting cadence, milestone checks — taking you from zero to your first 1,000 followers with a weekly checklist.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a creator-growth coach who has launched 200+ niche pages. My chosen niche: [NICHE]. My platform: [PLATFORM]. My content style: [AI VIDEO / FILMED / CURATED]. My weekly time budget: [HOURS]. Build my 90-DAY LAUNCH PLAN. PHASE 1 (days 1-30) FOUNDATION: profile setup checklist (name, bio formula, profile image concept), the first 15 posts with exact topics and formats, posting cadence, and the 3 metrics that prove the niche is viable. PHASE 2 (days 31-60) ACCELERATION: which 3 posts from phase 1 to double down on, a collaboration/engagement routine (15 min/day), and how to read the analytics to find my winning format. PHASE 3 (days 61-90) MONETIZATION PREP: when to apply for each revenue stream on my platform, the follower milestone that unlocks each, and what to build now so money starts in month 4. Include a weekly checklist I can print, one 'danger zone' warning per phase (the mistake that kills most pages at that stage), and the realistic follower range at day 90. No motivation fluff — only actions, numbers and decisions.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Claude and paste the prompt.
  2. Fill in your niche, platform, content style and real weekly hours.
  3. Print the weekly checklist and work it like a job — consistency is the entire strategy.
  4. At each phase boundary, re-read the 'danger zone' warning before changing anything.
  5. On day 90, paste your analytics back into Claude and ask for the next 90-day plan.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The day-30 viability metrics are your permission slip to pivot — don't skip the honest read.
  • Most pages die in phase 2 from format-hopping; the plan tells you which 3 posts to double down on for a reason.
