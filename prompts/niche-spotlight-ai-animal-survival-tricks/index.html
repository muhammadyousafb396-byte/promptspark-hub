Cinematic wildlife documentary scene, unbelievable but 100% real, [SURVIVAL TRICK: a wood frog frozen solid inside a crystal block of ice in a snowy forest, frost sparkling in bright winter light / a glowing immortal jellyfish drifting in deep blue ocean water, soft bioluminescent light / a hagfish releasing a huge cloud of glowing protective slime in deep blue ocean water / a pink axolotl regrowing a tiny new limb in a bright turquoise aquarium], vivid colour, dramatic close-up, jaw-dropping wonder, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Generate one survival trick in Veo 3 per video, swapping only [SURVIVAL TRICK] — one impossible-sounding trick per video keeps the 'sounds fake' hook clean; never list five tricks in one clip.
  2. Open the script with the fake-sounding claim first ('This frog freezes solid — and comes back to life') then prove it with the science in 2 lines — claim first, proof second is what holds viewers to the end.
  3. Voice it in ElevenLabs with an amazed documentary tone — survival tricks win on wonder; a flat voice makes a miracle sound like a textbook.
  4. End every video with 'Which trick shocked you most?' + a second trick teased for Part 2 — the tease turns one viral video into a series viewers follow.
  5. Post daily at 7-9 PM PKT and series it by animal (Frog Week, Ocean Week) — themed weeks turn curiosity into a daily habit.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: say the impossible thing in the first 3 seconds ('This animal can survive being frozen') — if the claim sounds fake, viewers stay to see the proof.
  • Caption trick: 'Sounds fake. It's 100% real. 🐸' — the fake-vs-real tension is the whole niche; put it in every caption.
  • Comment bait: end with 'Fake or real? Comment before the reveal' — debate comments push TikTok to show the video to more people.
