Kawaii Food Mascots — Midjourney Prompt for Cute Facebook Sticker Pages

Adorable kawaii food mascot character lineup, chibi style: a smiling sushi roll with rosy cheeks, a blushing bubble-tea cup with a straw arm waving, a happy croissant wearing a tiny chef hat, a giggling strawberry milk carton. Soft pastel background with floating sparkles, big glossy eyes, thick clean outlines, sticker-sheet composition with 4 characters in a 2x2 grid, glossy 3D render, ultra cute, vibrant candy colors --ar 1:1 --v 6

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Midjourney and paste the full prompt including the --ar 1:1 --v 6 parameters.
  2. Generate 4 grids and pick the one with the most consistent art style.
  3. Upscale your favorite grid, then crop each character into its own square.
  4. Remove the backgrounds to turn each mascot into a sticker or page avatar.
  5. Post one mascot per day as a 'meet the crew' reveal series.
  6. Run a 'name the mascot' contest in the comments once all four are revealed.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Sticker-style cute characters are Facebook's most-shared image format — saves and shares beat likes here.
  • Keep the same eye style and outline thickness across every character or the set won't feel like a family.
