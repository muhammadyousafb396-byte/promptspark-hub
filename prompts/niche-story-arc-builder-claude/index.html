Niche Story Arc Builder — Claude Prompt to Turn Any Niche Into a 10-Part Story Series

Ask Claude to wrap any niche — cooking, history, pets, money — in a 10-part story arc with cliffhangers, so casual viewers binge the whole series instead of watching one video and leaving.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a series architect for faceless creators. I will give you my niche at the end of this prompt. Turn it into a 10-part story series for YouTube Shorts or TikTok where each episode ends on a cliffhanger that makes viewers watch the next one. First, ask me (in your reply, list the questions) for: my niche, my audience, and whether I use AI video, real filming or screen recording. Then, once I answer, build: (1) the series premise in one gripping sentence, (2) the hero or recurring character/object viewers will follow, (3) all 10 episode titles with a 2-line synopsis each, (4) the cliffhanger ending for episodes 1-9, (5) the payoff episode 10 must deliver, (6) a recurring opening line and closing line for the whole series, (7) 3 comment-bait questions that run across the series. Keep every episode under 60 seconds of story. My niche: [PASTE YOUR NICHE HERE]. Rules: no episode may repeat another episode's twist, and the arc must work even if a viewer starts at episode 4.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy the full prompt above, replace [PASTE YOUR NICHE HERE] with your niche, and paste it into Claude.
  2. Answer Claude's 3 setup questions honestly — the arc only fits if the audience and format are real, not guessed.
  3. Read the 10-episode arc and check the cliffhangers: if episode 5's ending would not make YOU tap next, ask Claude to rewrite that one ending only.
  4. Produce episodes 1-3 before posting anything — a series with no backlog dies the moment episode 1 goes viral and episode 2 is not ready.
  5. Post one episode per day at the same time, and pin a playlist or series link on every episode so late joiners can binge from episode 1.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Start viewers mid-arc: post episode 1 with the caption 'Part 1 — wait for the ending' and episode 4 will still hook new viewers, because Claude designed each episode to stand alone.
  • Recycle the arc: once a 10-part series finishes, ask Claude to build 'Season 2' with the same hero but a new mystery — returning viewers are your cheapest growth.
  • Measure the arc, not the video: if episode-to-episode retention beats your channel average by 20 percent, the story format works — scale it to every niche you run.
