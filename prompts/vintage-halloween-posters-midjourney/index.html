Vintage Halloween Movie Poster Pack — Midjourney Prompt for Facebook October Pages

Retro 1950s-style Halloween movie posters — grinning pumpkins, haunted drive-ins and friendly ghosts — a Facebook image pack built for October shares.

AI ToolMidjourney
CategoryImage Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

Vintage 1950s Halloween movie poster style, [POSTER SUBJECT: a grinning jack-o-lantern driving a classic convertible past a haunted drive-in theater under a huge full moon / a friendly ghost family having a picnic on a hill of candy corn at dusk / a black cat in a bow tie conducting an orchestra of skeletons in a pumpkin patch / a witch on a bicycle delivering candy under streetlights in a small autumn town], bold retro typography space at top, aged paper texture, rich orange and teal palette, playful spooky charm, highly detailed illustration, 4:5 portrait

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Paste the prompt into Midjourney and pick ONE [POSTER SUBJECT] per image — one poster per post keeps the pack collectible.
  2. Generate a set of 6 posters with the same retro style (same paper texture, same palette) so the page looks like a real vintage series.
  3. Upscale your favorite, add the movie-style title in a retro font in any editor, and post one poster per day through October.
  4. Caption each poster like a real movie listing ('Now showing: The Pumpkin Drive-In') — the joke is the share engine.
  5. Post daily at 7 PM PKT and pin a comment asking 'Which one should be a real movie?' — votes drive comments and reach.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: the poster must read at thumbnail size — one big character, one big moon, nothing else competing.
  • Caption trick: fake showtimes ('Showing nightly until Halloween!') outperform plain descriptions — roleplay beats promotion.
  • Series trick: number the posters (Poster 04 of 06) — collectors save numbered sets far more than random images.
