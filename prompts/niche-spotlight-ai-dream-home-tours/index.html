AI Dream Home Tours (Instagram Niche)

Impossible AI-generated luxury homes — floating mansions, cave villas, treehouse estates — the most-saved content format on Reels. Is niche ki posts 148.3M/121.9M/103.7M/84.5M views tak gayi hain.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Cinematic architectural photograph, [HOME: a glass mansion floating above a waterfall at sunset, infinity pool merging into the sky / a luxury villa carved inside a glowing cave with a turquoise underground pool, lanterns and tropical plants / an extravagant multi-level treehouse mansion at dusk, glowing windows, rope bridges / a futuristic glass dome home in a desert at twilight, purple-orange sky, stars appearing], [LIGHTING DETAIL: dramatic sunset clouds / warm lantern glow / magical dusk light / violet twilight], ultra-luxury interior design visible, 8k architectural photography, dramatic composition --ar 4:5 --style raw

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Generate 4-6 dream-home images in Midjourney with the master prompt above, swapping only [HOME] each time.
  2. Animate each image in Runway or Kling (slow push-in, 5 seconds each) to turn them into Reels-ready clips.
  3. Build a 20-30s Reel: 4 homes, one on-screen hook: 'Which one would you pick? 🏡'
  4. Caption with a question + 3 luxury hashtags + #aidreamhome for discovery.
  5. Post daily at 7-9 PM; reply to every 'where is this?' comment with 'All AI — which should I build next?'

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: lead the Reel with the MOST impossible home — saves happen in the first 3 seconds.
  • Title trick: 'Houses that don't exist (yet)' outperforms 'dream homes' because of the curiosity gap.
  • Repost the same 4 homes as a carousel — carousels get 2x the saves of Reels in this niche.
