Lighthouse Through Four Seasons Timelapse — Sora Prompt for Cozy YouTube Niche Channels

One lighthouse, four seasons: snow, blossom, summer storms and autumn fog roll past the same lonely tower in a hypnotic timelapse — a calm, bingeable format for cozy and mystery channels alike.

AI ToolSora
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

Hypnotic seasonal timelapse, a lone striped lighthouse on a rocky cliff filmed from one fixed viewpoint as the year turns [SEASON MOMENT: heavy snow swirling around the tower as waves crash and the lamp glows amber through the blizzard / cherry blossom petals drifting past the lighthouse on a soft spring morning, calm blue sea below / a summer thunderstorm rolling in, lightning forking behind the tower as rain sweeps the cliff / thick autumn fog swallowing the rocks, the lamp beam cutting slow circles through the mist], same framing every season, cinematic color, peaceful and mysterious, 16:9, photorealistic

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Paste the prompt into Sora and pick ONE [SEASON MOMENT] per clip — one season per video keeps the timelapse readable; the fixed viewpoint is what sells the year passing.
  2. Lock the camera framing in every generation (same cliff, same tower position) so the four clips cut together as one seamless year.
  3. Generate all four seasons, then stitch them into one 60-90 second video with gentle crossfades and a calm ambient soundtrack.
  4. Add a soft narrator line per season ('Winter came first...') or let the music carry it — seasonal timelapses win on calm, not on script.
  5. Post on Sunday evenings when cozy audiences wind down, and pin a comment asking which season viewers would live in — season debates drive long comment threads.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: start the video on the most dramatic season (the thunderstorm) — calm openings lose scrollers; drama first, calm after.
  • Consistency trick: repeat the exact lighthouse description word for word in every generation — Sora drifts less when the anchor subject never changes.
  • Loop trick: end the autumn fog on a frame that matches the winter snow opening — a perfect loop doubles watch time on Shorts and Reels.
