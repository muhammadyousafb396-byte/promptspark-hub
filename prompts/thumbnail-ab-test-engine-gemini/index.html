Thumbnail A/B Test Engine — Gemini Prompt for YouTube CTR in Any Niche

Stop guessing thumbnails — feed Gemini your title and two thumbnail ideas and get a scored verdict on which one wins the click, plus 5 better variants. The CTR system every niche channel needs.

AI ToolGemini
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

Act as a YouTube CTR specialist who has tested 10,000+ thumbnails. My video title is: [YOUR TITLE]. My niche is: [YOUR NICHE]. Here are my two thumbnail ideas: (A) [DESCRIBE THUMBNAIL A — main subject, text, colors] and (B) [DESCRIBE THUMBNAIL B]. Score each thumbnail 1-10 on: (1) scroll-stopping power at small size, (2) curiosity gap, (3) emotion, (4) text readability, (5) niche fit. Declare a winner with the exact reason, then generate 5 NEW thumbnail concepts that beat both — each with: the main visual, the exact 2-4 word text, the color scheme, and why it would out-click A and B. Be blunt — if both my ideas are weak, say so and show me what a winner looks like.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Gemini and paste the prompt above, filling in your title, niche and two thumbnail ideas.
  2. Take the winner and the best of the 5 new concepts into your thumbnail tool.
  3. Make both thumbnails and run YouTube's Test & Compare if you have access — data beats opinion.
  4. Track CTR for 7 days, then ask Gemini to iterate on the winner: 'Beat this thumbnail.'
  5. Save every winning concept pattern — after 20 tests you will know your niche's CTR formula by heart.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Test ONE thing at a time — change the face or the text, never both, or you learn nothing.
  • Small-size check first: if the thumbnail is unreadable at phone size, no score matters.
