30-Episode Viral Series Bible — Claude Prompt for Creator Niches

Claude writes your complete series bible: a repeatable format, 30 episode concepts, hooks and cliffhangers — so your niche page never faces a blank page again. Series, not singles, build channels.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a showrunner for viral short-form series. Build me a 30-EPISODE SERIES BIBLE. My niche: [NICHE]. Platform: [PLATFORM]. Format: [AI VIDEO / FACELESS NARRATION / ON-CAMERA]. Episode length: [30-60 SECONDS]. Include: 1) SERIES PREMISE in one sentence a stranger instantly understands. 2) THE REPEATABLE FORMAT: the fixed opening beat (first 2 seconds, identical every episode), the middle structure, and the closing cliffhanger or callback that makes viewers follow for the next episode. 3) 30 EPISODE CONCEPTS in a table: episode number, title (under 50 characters, keyword-first), the exact opening hook line, the payoff, and which earlier episode it calls back to. 4) CHARACTER/RUNNING GAGS: 3 recurring elements that reward loyal viewers. 5) POSTING RHYTHM: the days, order and pinned-comment strategy for launching the series, plus the 3 metrics that tell me by episode 10 whether to extend to 100 episodes or kill it. Make every episode producible in under one hour with my format. No filler episodes — each one must earn its slot.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Claude and paste the prompt with your niche, platform and format filled in.
  2. Read the repeatable format section twice — consistency of the opening beat is what makes a series bingeable.
  3. Produce episodes in the given order; the callbacks are sequenced on purpose.
  4. After episode 10, paste your real metrics into Claude and ask for the extend-or-kill decision from section 5.
  5. If it extends, ask Claude for episodes 31-100 using the same bible — never change the format mid-series.

💡 Pro Tips

  • A series bible turns content from daily invention into daily execution — batch-film 5 episodes in one sitting.
  • The pinned comment should always link the previous episode; binge chains are where series channels actually grow.
