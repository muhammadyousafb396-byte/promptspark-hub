Robot Chef Comedy Fails — Veo 3 Prompt for Viral TikTok Niche Pages

A proud robot chef whose cooking goes hilariously wrong — pancake on the ceiling, spaghetti fountain — short slapstick AI comedy built for TikTok niche pages and YouTube Shorts series.

AI ToolVeo 3
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyBeginner

📋 Copy the Prompt

Hilarious photorealistic slapstick clip of a proud shiny white robot chef in a bright modern kitchen attempting to flip a pancake, the pancake flies up and sticks flat to the ceiling, the robot looks up slowly with blinking surprised LED eyes, then shrugs with perfect comedic timing while a second pancake lands on its own head like a hat, warm cheerful kitchen lighting, flour dust floating in sunbeams, locked-off camera at counter height, expressive robot body language, family-friendly physical comedy, 9:16 vertical, ultra detailed, no dialogue text, no watermark

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Veo 3 and start a new text-to-video generation.
  2. Paste the prompt exactly — the beat order (flip, ceiling, shrug, hat) is the joke structure.
  3. Set 9:16 vertical for TikTok and Shorts comedy niches.
  4. Generate 2-3 takes and pick the one with the clearest comedic pause before the shrug.
  5. Add a boing sound effect on the ceiling stick and post as episode 1 of 'Robot Chef Fails'.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Number every episode — series numbering turns one funny clip into a follow-worthy TikTok niche page.
  • Ask viewers 'what should he cook next?' — the top comment becomes your next episode and pre-sells its views.
