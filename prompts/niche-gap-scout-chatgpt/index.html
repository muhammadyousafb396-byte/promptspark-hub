Niche Gap Scout — ChatGPT Prompt to Find Rising 2026 Content Niches

ChatGPT hunts rising content gaps on your platform — niches with surging search demand and weak competition — and ranks 10 by opportunity score. Launch where the crowd isn't looking yet.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a content-market research analyst with access to 2026 platform trends. My platform: [PLATFORM: YouTube / TikTok / Facebook / Instagram]. My skills or interests: [SKILLS]. My format preference: [FACELESS / ON-CAMERA / AI-GENERATED]. Do this in 4 parts. PART 1 — scan for RISING GAPS: identify 10 niches on my platform where audience demand is growing but creator supply is still thin, and for each give: the niche name, the search trend signal (what people are typing), 3 example channels/pages proving demand, and why incumbents are weak. PART 2 — score each niche 1-10 on: demand growth, competition level, monetization potential, and fit with my skills. PART 3 — rank the top 3 and give each a 30-day validation test: 10 video ideas, the one metric to watch, and the kill criteria (when to quit). PART 4 — name the niche everyone THINKS is hot but is actually saturated in 2026, and explain why latecomers lose there. Be specific — no generic 'fitness' or 'finance' without a sub-niche angle.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT and paste the prompt.
  2. Fill in your platform, skills and format preference honestly — the ranking depends on it.
  3. Read Part 4 first so you don't accidentally pick the saturated 'hot' niche.
  4. Run the 30-day validation test on your top-ranked niche before committing.
  5. Re-run this quarterly — niche gaps close fast, and new ones open with every platform update.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The kill criteria in Part 3 are the real value — most creators quit winners too early and ride losers too long.
  • Ask ChatGPT to compare the same niche across two platforms; the winner is usually the one with less competition.
