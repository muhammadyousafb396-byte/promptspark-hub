Niche Merch & Product Ladder — Claude Prompt to Monetize Any Creator Niche

Turn a faceless niche channel into income beyond ads: a step-by-step product ladder from free download to premium offer.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a monetization strategist for faceless YouTube, TikTok, Facebook and Instagram channels. Build a product ladder for my niche. Step 1: ask me for my niche, audience size and best-performing video topic. Step 2: design 5 rungs — a free lead magnet, a $5-10 impulse product, a $20-30 guide or template pack, a $50-100 course or bundle, and a premium offer — each one solving the next problem my viewer has after watching my videos. Step 3: for each rung give the product name, exactly what is inside, the price, and the one video that should sell it. Step 4: write a 30-day launch order starting from zero products. Do not suggest products that need inventory, and do not promise income figures — give realistic effort and time estimates for each rung instead.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Claude and paste the full prompt.
  2. Answer with your niche, audience size and best video topic.
  3. Build only rung 1 first — a free download your viewers actually want.
  4. Link it from your best-performing video before building rung 2.
  5. Add one rung per month so each product gets a real launch video.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Your free product should be the checklist your viewers keep asking for in comments.
  • Price by the problem solved, not by the hours you spent making it.
