Lighthouse Keeper Storm POV — Sora Prompt for Faceless YouTube Horror Niches

First-person storm footage triggers primal awe: a lighthouse lantern room during a night storm delivers cinematic terror with zero filming, zero actors, zero budget.

AI ToolSora
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

First-person POV from inside a lighthouse lantern room during a violent night storm. Rain hammers the curved glass in sheets, lightning splits the black sky and silhouettes towering waves crashing against the rocks below. The giant rotating lamp sweeps its beam across the chaos, the camera slowly creaking with the rotation to follow the light. A distant ship silhouette struggles in the swell. Salt mist blasts the windows, an oil lamp flickers warm inside the room. Gothic maritime horror atmosphere, oppressive and beautiful, ultra-realistic storm cinematography, 9:16 vertical, no people, no dialogue.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Sora, paste the prompt, set 9:16 and 10 seconds.
  2. Generate 4-5 clips with the same 'lighthouse storm' seed words for consistency.
  3. Layer real storm audio (rain, thunder, creaking metal) under the clips.
  4. Chain clips into a 45-60s Short with escalating lightning strikes as the arc.
  5. Title it like found footage: 'The keeper vanished in 1987 — this was his last shift'.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Never explain the story in the video — found-footage mystery doubles comment rates.
  • Add one jump-frame (a shadow passing the glass at 0:32) for replay value.
  • The same Sora workflow works for 'abandoned fire lookout' or 'ship bridge' variants.
