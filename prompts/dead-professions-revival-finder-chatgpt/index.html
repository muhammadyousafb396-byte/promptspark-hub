Dead Professions Revival Finder — ChatGPT Prompt to Find Forgotten-Job Creator Niches

Mine forgotten jobs — lamplighters, switchboard operators, town criers — for faceless history and craft channels nobody else is making.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a YouTube niche researcher specialising in forgotten professions. Find 15 professions that have mostly or completely disappeared in the last 150 years. For each profession, give: (1) the job title and the years it existed, (2) a one-sentence description of what a day in the job looked like, (3) the single most surprising or dramatic fact about it, (4) a faceless video idea for it with a curiosity-gap title under 60 characters, (5) a score from 1-10 for visual potential and a score from 1-10 for story potential, and (6) whether it suits YouTube long-form, Shorts, TikTok or Facebook Reels best. Rank the 15 by combined score. Exclude jobs that are already saturated on YouTube, such as blacksmiths and lighthouse keepers. End with your top 3 picks and a 30-day launch plan for the number one pick: 12 video titles in posting order.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT and paste the full prompt above exactly as written.
  2. Read the ranked list and shortlist the 3 professions with the highest combined visual and story scores.
  3. Ask ChatGPT to expand your chosen profession into 10 episode titles with a one-line hook for each.
  4. Check the top 3 ideas on YouTube search to confirm the niche is genuinely underserved before committing.
  5. Pick one profession, publish the 12-video launch plan, and keep the other two as backup channels.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Ask for professions from one country or era at a time — 'Victorian London' or '1950s America' gives far more specific, filmable ideas than a global list.
  • The surprising fact is the video: build every script around fact number 3, not around the job description.
