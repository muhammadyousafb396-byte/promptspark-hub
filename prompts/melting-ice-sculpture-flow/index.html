Melting Ice Sculpture Reveal — Google Flow Prompt for Satisfying Facebook Reels

A crystal ice sculpture melts in warm light to reveal a glowing treasure hidden inside — a satisfying reveal loop that earns replays, 'what is inside?' comments and effortless Facebook shares.

AI ToolGoogle Flow
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyBeginner

📋 Copy the Prompt

Satisfying reveal video, a crystal-clear ice sculpture on a dark stone pedestal slowly melting under warm golden light [REVEAL MOMENT: an ice swan melting to reveal a glowing ruby heart beating softly inside / an ice castle dripping away to uncover a tiny golden key frozen at its center / an ice rose melting petal by petal to reveal a sparkling diamond at its core / an ice dragon dissolving into mist to free a glowing pearl], slow drips, sparkling meltwater, dramatic warm-versus-cold light, crisp detail, hypnotic pacing, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Paste the prompt into Google Flow and pick ONE [REVEAL MOMENT] per clip — one hidden treasure per Reel keeps the guessing game alive to the last second.
  2. Keep the pedestal, lighting and camera angle identical across the series so viewers learn the format and stay for the reveal they know is coming.
  3. Generate the melt in one continuous take if possible, or cut melt-start to reveal in two clips with a seamless drip transition.
  4. Add a soft dripping-water sound and a rising musical swell at the reveal — the sound swell is what makes viewers feel the payoff.
  5. Post daily at 7-9 PM PKT and never spoil the treasure in the caption — 'Wait for what is inside...' comments outperform any description.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: the first frame must show the sculpture already glistening and dripping — a dry ice block reads as a photo; visible melt proves motion.
  • Patience trick: make the reveal arrive in the final 2 seconds — late payoffs earn full watches and replays, which Facebook rewards heavily.
  • Series trick: number the sculptures (Sculpture 07: The Swan) — collectors follow numbered reveal series to the end, and the numbering itself becomes the brand.
