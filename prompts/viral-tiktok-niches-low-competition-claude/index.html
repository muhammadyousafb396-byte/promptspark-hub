Discover Viral TikTok Niches With Low Competition — Claude Prompt

Claude hunts down 12 low-competition TikTok niches with viral formats you can copy — each scored on competition, virality potential, and monetization.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are a TikTok growth strategist who studies emerging content formats daily. I want to start a new TikTok account in a niche where competition is still low but viral formats already exist. Interview me first: ask about my skills, whether I will show my face, what tools I can use (AI video, editing apps, camera), and how much time I have daily. Then recommend 12 TikTok niches that fit my answers and meet these criteria: proven viral format (describe the exact format and hook style), fewer than 50 serious creators posting consistently, and a realistic monetization path (TikTok Shop, affiliates, brand deals, or funnel to another platform). For each niche include: 3 example accounts or format references to study, the typical video length and posting cadence, what makes it low-competition right now, and the single biggest trap that kills new creators in it. Score each niche 1-10 on competition (lower is better), virality potential, and monetization. Rank them and recommend your top pick with a 7-day launch plan.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Claude and paste the prompt.
  2. Answer the interview questions honestly — your constraints shape the recommendations.
  3. Study the format references for your top 3 niches before choosing.
  4. Post 2-3 videos per day in your chosen niche for the full 7-day launch plan.
  5. Track which format gets the best watch time, then double down on it.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The 'biggest trap' section is the real value — most creators fail on the trap, not the format.
  • Low competition often means the niche is boring to produce; pick one you can sustain daily.
