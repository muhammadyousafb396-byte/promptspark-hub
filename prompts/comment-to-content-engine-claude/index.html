Comment-to-Content Engine — Claude Prompt for Viral Video Ideas

Claude turns your comment section into an endless video-idea machine — mining questions, debates and requests into a ranked, ready-to-film content pipeline.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are an audience-mining strategist. Turn my COMMENTS into CONTENT. I will paste my recent comments below. My niche: [NICHE]. Platform: [PLATFORM]. Do this: 1) CLUSTER the comments into 5-7 themes: questions, debates, requests, praise, complaints. 2) For each theme, write 3 video concepts that answer or exploit it, each with a title under 50 characters and a one-line hook. 3) RANK all concepts by a simple score: comment volume times emotional intensity times ease of production. 4) Write the top 5 as ready-to-film briefs: the hook for the first 3 seconds, the 3 beats of the video, and the call-to-action that generates MORE comments. 5) Give me a weekly comment-mining routine: 20 minutes, exact steps, so the pipeline never runs dry. Comments to analyze: [PASTE COMMENTS].

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Copy 30-50 recent comments from your best-performing videos.
  2. Open Claude, paste the prompt, then paste your comments where indicated.
  3. Film the top-ranked brief first — it is literally what your audience asked for.
  4. Reply to the original commenters when the video ships to seed early engagement.
  5. Repeat weekly; your comment section compounds into an idea asset.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Debate-theme comments make the best videos — disagreement drives watch time and replies.
  • Even with 50 followers this works; small audiences leave the most honest comments.
