Infinite Zoom Illusion Clips — Google Flow Prompt for Viral Shorts

A mind-bending infinite zoom — from a galaxy down to a city street, into a coffee cup, down to the molecules — the loop-style illusion that racks up rewatches on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Seamless infinite-zoom illusion video: start on a swirling spiral galaxy, dive through starfields into a glowing night-time city, sweep down one lit street, plunge through a cafe window, drop into a steaming coffee cup, keep diving into the swirling latte art until the foam patterns become a new galaxy — one continuous unbroken zoom with no cuts, smooth accelerating motion, glowing edges and light trails guiding the eye, hypnotic and perfectly loopable, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic with subtle sci-fi glow, ultra detailed, no text, no watermark

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Google Flow and start a new text-to-video generation.
  2. Paste the prompt exactly — the 'no cuts' and 'continuous zoom' phrases are critical to the illusion.
  3. Generate 3-4 variations and scrub frame by frame to find the smoothest unbroken zoom.
  4. In your editor, duplicate and reverse-copy the clip so it loops perfectly end to start.
  5. Post with 'watch till the end' in the caption — loops double average watch time on Shorts.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Change the final object each video (galaxy → coffee, galaxy → eye, galaxy → city) so the series stays fresh.
  • Add a rising ambient drone sound — audio buildup makes viewers stay through the full zoom.
