📋 Copy the Prompt

Punchy, curiosity-driving body-facts video, glowing anatomical visuals and bright science imagery, [BODY FACT: why your eye twitches when you are tired, shown as a sparkling close-up of an eye / why you jerk awake just as you fall asleep, shown as a glowing brain firing at sunset / what your gut tells your brain before you feel hungry, shown as golden light travelling between stomach and brain / why your heart beats faster when you see someone you like, shown as a glowing heart hologram beating with energy], one jaw-dropping body fact per video, the 'wait, really?' moment in the first 3 seconds, rapid-fire energetic voiceover, 9:16 vertical, 30-60 seconds

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Generate one body fact in Veo 3 per video, swapping only [BODY FACT] — one fact, one glowing visual, one 'aha' per video keeps the curiosity clean; never explain three body systems in one clip.
  2. Script each video in ChatGPT as question + visual reveal + simple explanation: ask the question about the viewer's own body, show the glowing visual, then explain it in one plain sentence — the body is the star; a textbook list is not.
  3. Open on the impossible-looking visual in the first 3 seconds (the sparkling eye, the firing brain, the beating heart hologram) — a body mystery stops the TikTok scroll; a definition does not.
  4. Voice it in ElevenLabs with an energetic, curious, slightly amazed tone — body facts win on wonder; a monotone voice makes the most amazing fact sound boring.
  5. Post daily at 7-9 PM PKT as a series — 'Body Fact 01, 02, 03' — numbered facts turn one viral video into a daily curiosity show viewers follow and return to.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: ask the question about the viewer's own body first ('Why does your eye twitch when you are tired?') — a question about yourself hooks faster than any fact about strangers.
  • Caption trick: 'Tag someone whose eye twitches' — the tag turns viewers into sharers naming a friend, which pushes the video further.
  • Trust rule: stick to well-known curiosity facts and never give medical advice or diagnose anyone — curiosity earns saves, health claims earn trouble.
