Animated Quote Kinetic Reels — Runway Prompt for Instagram Theme Pages

Kinetic typography quote videos — bold words slamming in over cinematic backgrounds. The stoic/motivation theme-page format that gets saved and shared.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Vertical kinetic typography video for an Instagram motivation page: a dramatic cinematic background of [BACKGROUND: storm clouds breaking over a mountain peak / waves crashing on black rocks at dawn / a lone figure walking a desert road at sunset], bold condensed white typography animating in word by word with punchy staggered timing, each word scaling up slightly on entry then settling, subtle film grain and a dark vignette keeping text readable, smooth ease-out motion, the full quote revealed across 6 seconds then a final hold with a soft light pulse behind the last word, deep moody color grade with one accent color, 9:16 vertical, clean legible type, no watermark, professional motion-design feel

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Runway and paste the prompt into a text-to-video or Gen-4 project.
  2. Choose 9:16 vertical before generating.
  3. Generate, then check the lettering — if Runway mangles text, overlay your real quote in your editor (common fix).
  4. Pair with a trending audio track at low volume under the motion.
  5. Post 1 per day with niche hashtags and a save-worthy caption.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Lock ONE font and ONE accent color across all videos — theme-page branding is what converts followers.
  • 8-12 word quotes perform best; longer ones get skipped before the payoff.
