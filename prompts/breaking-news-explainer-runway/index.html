Breaking-News Explainer Loops — Runway Prompt for Viral Facebook Pages

Cinematic 'breaking news' style B-roll loops — spinning globes, data streams, city skylines at dawn — the authoritative motion background Facebook explainer pages loop behind their narration.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Sleek broadcast-news style motion graphic: a slowly rotating glass globe with glowing data arcs connecting continents, holographic numbers and charts rising in the foreground, deep navy and electric-blue studio backdrop with subtle light flares, a sunrise city skyline fading in behind the globe, smooth seamless loop, camera pushes in slowly, premium newsroom aesthetic, 16:9, crisp motion, ultra detailed, no text, no watermark, no real logos

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Runway and start a text-to-video generation.
  2. Paste the prompt and generate a 10-second clip — the loopable design means one clip covers a full voiceover.
  3. Export the clip and loop it 4-6 times under your narrated script in any free editor.
  4. Record or generate a 30-60 second voiceover explaining one news fact per video.
  5. Post with a news-style title and pin the source link in comments — Facebook rewards informative video hard.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Pick one beat (tech, money, space) — pages that cover 'everything news' grow slower than focused ones.
  • Regenerate the globe with different accent colors per series so regular viewers recognize your brand instantly.
