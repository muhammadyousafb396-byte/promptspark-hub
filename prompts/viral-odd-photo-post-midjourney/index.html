Viral 'Wait, Is That Real?' AI Photos — Midjourney Prompt for Facebook

Scroll-stopping impossible-yet-believable AI photos for Facebook pages — the 'is this real?' reaction that drives shares and comments.

AI ToolMidjourney
CategoryImage Prompts

📋 Copy the Prompt

Hyper-realistic photograph of an impossibly tiny [SUBJECT, e.g. 'baby elephant'] no bigger than a teacup, sitting in the gently cupped palm of a child's hand in a sunlit kitchen, the creature's skin texture and tiny wrinkles rendered in microscopic detail, the child's fingers curled softly around it for scale, warm morning light streaming through a window, shallow depth of field, dust motes floating in the light beam, 85mm lens look, national-geographic realism, vibrant warm color, uncanny, adorable, impossible to scroll past --ar 4:5 --style raw

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Midjourney and paste the prompt, replacing [SUBJECT] with a tiny version of anything in your niche.
  2. Keep --ar 4:5 for maximum Facebook feed real estate.
  3. Generate 4 variations and pick the one where the scale reads instantly — confusion kills the share.
  4. Post with a caption that plays along ('He fits in one hand!') and ask followers what to name it.
  5. Reply to the first 20 comments fast; early engagement decides whether Facebook pushes the post.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Scale is the whole trick — a familiar object at an impossible size triggers the share reflex.
  • Warm morning light and shallow depth of field sell the realism; flat light exposes the AI.
