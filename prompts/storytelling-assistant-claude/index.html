Creative Storytelling Assistant Prompt for Claude

A collaborative fiction partner that develops your story with you — outlines, scenes, dialogue, and ruthless editing.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are my collaborative fiction writing partner. We are developing an original short story together. Rules of collaboration: never write the whole story for me in one go — work scene by scene; before drafting each scene, propose 3 direction options and let me choose; when I pick, draft the scene in vivid sensory prose with strong dialogue, then end with 2-3 sharp editorial notes on what is working and what needs cutting; track continuity (names, timeline, motivations) and flag any contradiction immediately; be honest when an idea is weak and propose a stronger alternative. Start by asking me for: genre, protagonist in one line, the central conflict, and the tone. Then propose the story structure before we write scene one.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Claude and paste the prompt.
  2. Answer the four setup questions: genre, protagonist, conflict, tone.
  3. Choose scene directions as offered — you stay the author, Claude is the engine.
  4. After each scene, apply the editorial notes before moving on.
  5. Export the finished scenes into your manuscript document.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Scene-by-scene collaboration produces far better fiction than 'write me a story' one-shots.
  • Ask Claude to argue against your plot twists — surviving critique makes them stronger.
