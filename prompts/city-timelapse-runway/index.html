Day-to-Night City Timelapse Prompt for Runway

A hypnotic day-to-night skyline timelapse with traffic light trails — timelapses are evergreen high-retention content.

AI ToolRunway
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyBeginner

📋 Copy the Prompt

Stunning day-to-night timelapse of a modern city skyline viewed from across a river, the sun sets behind glass skyscrapers igniting the clouds in orange and pink, city lights flicker on one by one as deep blue twilight settles, car light trails streak along the riverside highway in long red and white ribbons, clouds race across the sky, stars emerging faintly overhead, water reflections shimmering, perfectly smooth accelerated motion with no flicker, cinematic color grade, ultra sharp, photorealistic, 16:9, no text, no watermark

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Runway and paste the prompt into text-to-video.
  2. Set 16:9 and the longest duration available for a full day-to-night arc.
  3. Watch for flicker between frames — Runway usually handles this well, but regenerate if lights strobe unnaturally.
  4. Add a slow cinematic score; timelapses pair beautifully with orchestral or lo-fi tracks.
  5. Use as an intro/outro background or a standalone Shorts post.

💡 Pro Tips

  • 'No flicker' and 'perfectly smooth accelerated motion' are the key phrases for clean timelapses.
  • River or water in the foreground doubles the visual interest through reflections.
