Niche Moat Builder — Claude Prompt to Design a Channel Angle Nobody Can Copy

A Claude strategy prompt that designs your channel's un-copyable moat — the signature format, character or world that makes YOUR niche impossible to clone, even when the niche itself is crowded.

AI ToolClaude
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

Act as a brand strategist for faceless creator channels. My niche: [YOUR NICHE, e.g. 'AI horror stories on YouTube']. My tools: [e.g. Sora, ElevenLabs, CapCut]. My constraints: [e.g. 2 hours/day, $50/month, no on-camera]. Design my channel's MOAT — the thing competitors cannot copy without looking like thieves. Deliver: (1) SIGNATURE FORMAT — a repeatable video structure unique to me (e.g. 'every story ends with the monster addressing the viewer by name'), with a template for episode 1, (2) SIGNATURE CHARACTER/WORLD — a recurring narrator, mascot or setting with a one-paragraph bible (appearance, voice, catchphrase, rules of the world), (3) SIGNATURE STYLE — the exact visual/audio fingerprint (color grade, font, music style, pacing) described precisely enough to brief an editor, (4) DEFENSIBILITY TEST — 5 ways a copycat would try to clone me and why each fails, (5) 90-DAY MOAT DEEPENING PLAN — what I add at day 30, 60 and 90 to make the moat wider (lore drops, community rituals, format twists). Rules: the moat must work faceless; it must be executable inside my constraints; no generic advice ('be consistent', 'find your voice') — every recommendation must be specific to MY niche.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Paste into Claude, fill in niche, tools and constraints honestly — the moat is only defensible if it's buildable inside YOUR limits.
  2. Read the defensibility test first: if you can imagine a copycat beating any of the 5 scenarios, iterate the prompt with that scenario as a new constraint.
  3. Build the signature character/world FIRST (part 2) — formats get copied in weeks, but a beloved character takes months to clone.
  4. Document the style fingerprint (part 3) as a one-page brief and hand it to every editor/VA — moats die when the style drifts.
  5. Execute the 90-day deepening plan on schedule: lore and rituals are what turn viewers into a community that defends you.
  6. Re-run quarterly with your analytics — the moat evolves as the niche matures and copycats get smarter.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The 'no generic advice' rule is load-bearing — without it, Claude writes motivational fluff; with it, you get a buildable strategy.
  • A moat is not a niche — the niche is WHERE you compete, the moat is WHY you win there; never confuse the two.
  • Start the community ritual by episode 5, not episode 50 — early rituals compound; late rituals feel forced.
