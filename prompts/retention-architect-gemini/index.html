Retention Architect — Gemini Prompt to Engineer High-Retention Scripts

Turn Gemini into a retention engineer that rewrites your scripts beat-by-beat for maximum watch time.

AI ToolGemini
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

Act as a video retention engineer who has studied thousands of viral short-form and long-form videos. I will paste my video script. Your job: 1) Score the script's retention strength from 1-100 with reasoning. 2) Rewrite the opening 3 seconds into 3 alternative hooks — each under 12 words, each creating an open loop. 3) Mark every line where viewers are likely to swipe away, and explain why. 4) Insert a pattern interrupt (visual cue, question, or twist) every 5-8 seconds in the rewrite. 5) Add one curiosity gap in the middle that pays off only at the end. 6) Rewrite the call-to-action so it feels like a natural continuation, not an ad. 7) Give me a second-by-second retention curve prediction for the rewritten script. My niche is [NICHE] and my platform is [YOUTUBE / TIKTOK / INSTAGRAM]. Here is my script: [PASTE SCRIPT]. Be ruthless — cut anything that doesn't earn the next second of attention.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Gemini and paste the full prompt above with your script.
  2. Fill in your niche and platform so the rewrite matches the format.
  3. Compare the original and rewritten retention curves.
  4. Apply the top 3 fixes even if you don't use the full rewrite.
  5. Film the 3 alternative hooks as separate videos and test which wins.
  6. Build a checklist from Gemini's feedback and apply it to every future script.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The 3-second hook test is the highest-leverage use — test hooks before filming the whole video.
  • Ask Gemini to explain each swipe-away point so you learn the pattern, not just the fix.
