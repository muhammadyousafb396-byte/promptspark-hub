Niche Crossover Machine — ChatGPT Prompt to Fuse Two Niches Into One Viral Channel

A niche-discovery engine that fuses two unrelated niches into one original channel concept with zero direct competition.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

Act as a viral-content strategist. I will give you TWO niches, and you will fuse them into 10 original channel concepts that have never existed. For each concept: 1) The fusion name and one-line premise. 2) Exactly why the crossover creates novelty (the psychological hook). 3) Three content series that could run forever. 4) The target audience overlap and where to find them. 5) A competition check — why no existing channel owns this. 6) Monetization paths unique to this crossover. My two niches are: [NICHE 1] and [NICHE 2]. Rank the 10 concepts from most to least viable for 2026, and for the top 3 give me 5 video titles each that would work as the launch videos.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT and paste the full prompt above.
  2. Fill in two niches you genuinely like — passion fuels consistency.
  3. Run the fusion twice with different niche pairs and compare outputs.
  4. Pick the top concept and ask ChatGPT for a 30-video launch calendar.
  5. Launch with the 5 recommended launch videos, one per day.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Fuse one big niche with one tiny one — the tiny one brings the novelty.
  • Ask ChatGPT to re-rank after you share your first month's analytics.
