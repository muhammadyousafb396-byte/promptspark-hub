AI Quiz & Trivia Videos (TikTok Niche)

Interactive quiz and trivia videos for TikTok — glowing question marks, puzzle brains and golden trophies that make viewers play along and comment their score. Is niche ki videos 212.8M/186.5M/159.3M/127.4M views tak gayi hain.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Exciting colorful quiz-show scene, [QUIZ MOMENT: a giant glowing golden question mark floating over a game-show stage with confetti and bright lights / a brain made of glowing colorful puzzle pieces on a dark stage under spotlights / a golden trophy on a podium surrounded by colorful balloons and confetti in a bright studio / mysterious glowing treasure chests and ancient artifacts in a dramatic quiz-show setting with moody lighting], vivid color, dramatic lighting, playful and curiosity-driving, 9:16 vertical, photorealistic

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Generate one quiz moment in Veo 3 per question, swapping only [QUIZ MOMENT] — the game-show visual is the hook; the question comes from the voiceover and on-screen text.
  2. Script each video in ChatGPT as 5 questions with a 5-second think timer: easy first, impossible last — the last question must be so hard that most viewers fail it, because failure drives comments.
  3. Voice it in ElevenLabs with a fast, playful game-show host tone — quiz videos live on energy and suspense; a slow voice makes a 5-second timer feel like a lecture.
  4. End every video with 'Comment your score out of 5' — the comment-your-score hook is the entire growth engine of this niche; never end on the answer, end on the challenge.
  5. Post 2x/day 12 PM and 7 PM PKT and series it by theme (geography week, logo week, riddle week) — themed series turn one viral video into a daily habit for viewers.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Hook rule: open on the hardest question first as a teaser ('Only 1% get this one') — quiz scrollers decide in 1 second whether they can beat the quiz.
  • Caption trick: 'I bet you cannot get 5/5' — a direct challenge caption outperforms any summary; viewers watch to prove the caption wrong.
  • Comment bait: pin 'What did you score? Be honest!' — scores are the comment engine, and TikTok pushes videos whose comments grow fastest.
