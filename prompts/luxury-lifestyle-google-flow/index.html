Luxury Yacht Lifestyle Video Prompt for Google Flow

A cinematic superyacht sunset scene dripping with luxury — aspirational lifestyle content that brands pay for.

AI ToolGoogle Flow
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyAdvanced

📋 Copy the Prompt

Aspirational luxury lifestyle film: a gleaming white superyacht anchored off a tropical island at sunset, champagne being poured in slow motion on the aft deck with the golden liquid catching the light, an infinity pool on the deck mirroring the orange sky, camera begins with a wide aerial establishing shot then sweeps down to deck level in one fluid move, palm silhouettes on the island shore, gentle waves lapping the hull, warm cinematic color grade with rich golds and deep teals, filmic 24fps motion, photorealistic, ultra detailed, 16:9, no text, no people in close-up, no watermark, no brand logos

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Google Flow and build this as two connected scenes: aerial wide, then deck-level detail.
  2. Generate at 16:9 for YouTube luxury compilations or 9:16 for Reels.
  3. Keep people distant or absent — AI faces in luxury content break the fantasy fast.
  4. Add a motivational voiceover about success habits to turn views into followers.
  5. Pitch similar clips to travel and finance brands once your page grows.

💡 Pro Tips

  • 'No brand logos' protects you from trademark issues when monetizing luxury content.
  • The aerial-to-deck camera move is what separates amateur clips from film-like ones.
