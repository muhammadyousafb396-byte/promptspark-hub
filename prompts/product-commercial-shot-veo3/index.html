Luxury Product Commercial Shot Prompt for Veo 3

A high-end perfume commercial shot with slow-motion liquid and studio lighting — perfect for faceless product affiliate content.

AI ToolVeo 3
CategoryVideo Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

Luxury perfume commercial shot: a faceted crystal perfume bottle standing on wet black marble, a slow-motion splash of golden liquid arcs around it in mid-air, dramatic rim lighting outlines the glass in warm amber while a cool blue backlight creates depth, camera orbits slowly in a 45-degree arc at product eye level, micro-droplets suspended and sparkling, soft smoke drifting through the background, deep shadows, premium advertising aesthetic, hyper-detailed reflections, 120fps slow-motion feel, photorealistic, 16:9, no text, no hands, no watermark

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Veo 3 and paste the prompt, swapping 'perfume bottle' for your actual product if needed.
  2. Set 16:9 for YouTube ads or 9:16 for TikTok Shop / Reels product clips.
  3. Generate 3-4 takes; pick the orbit with the most elegant liquid motion.
  4. Add your product name as a lower-third graphic in editing — never bake text into the AI render.
  5. Use it as a B-roll hero shot in product reviews or affiliate videos.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Describe materials precisely ('faceted crystal', 'wet black marble') — Veo 3 renders premium materials far better with specific nouns.
  • For skincare or supplements, change the liquid color to match the brand palette.
