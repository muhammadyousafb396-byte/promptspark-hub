Keyword Gap Miner — ChatGPT Prompt to Find Underserved 2026 Content Ideas

ChatGPT hunts the questions your competitors rank for but answer badly — then hands you 20 content gaps you can win this month. Niche discovery for creators who want demand with proof.

AI ToolChatGPT
CategoryText Prompts
DifficultyIntermediate

📋 Copy the Prompt

You are an SEO content strategist who finds underserved search demand. I will give you my niche and 3 competitor channels/sites. Do this in 3 phases. PHASE 1 — ask me (one at a time): (1) my niche and sub-niche, (2) my platform — YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, or blog, (3) 3 competitors I want to outrank, (4) my format — long-form, Shorts/Reels, or posts. PHASE 2 — gap mining: list 20 content ideas where competitor coverage is thin, weak, or outdated. For EACH idea give: the exact search phrase people type, why current results fail the searcher (thin content, old info, wrong format), the searcher intent in one line, and a difficulty score 1-10. PHASE 3 — rank the 20 by the ratio of demand to difficulty, then give me the top 5 with: a working title under 60 characters, the angle that beats every current result, and the 3 sub-points that make mine the definitive answer. Rules: no generic 'ultimate guide' ideas, every idea must name a specific gap, be honest about which of my top 5 I could still lose and why.

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open ChatGPT and paste the prompt.
  2. Answer the 4 setup questions one at a time.
  3. Take the top 5 ideas and sanity-check each in actual search before committing.
  4. Produce the #1 idea first — it is your fastest win.
  5. Re-run monthly — gaps close as competitors catch up.

💡 Pro Tips

  • The 'why current results fail' column is the whole game — that sentence becomes your video's unique angle.
  • A difficulty score of 3 or under with real demand is a green light — ship within 7 days before someone else does.
