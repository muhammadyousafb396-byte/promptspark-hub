Spooky Halloween Portraits — Midjourney Prompt for Facebook

Gothic Halloween portraits — witches, haunted mansions and pumpkin kings in cinematic detail — built for Facebook posts that collect thousands of shares.

📋 Copy the Prompt

Gothic Halloween portrait of a mysterious witch in a Victorian dress standing in a moonlit pumpkin patch, twisted bare trees behind her, bats crossing a huge full moon, glowing jack-o'-lanterns at her feet, intricate lace and fabric detail, cinematic rim lighting, deep purples and burning oranges, hyper detailed, 8k --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6.1

🚀 How to Use This Prompt

  1. Open Midjourney and paste the full prompt including the --ar 4:5 --style raw --v 6.1 parameters.
  2. Generate 4 variations and upscale the one with the strongest moon-and-silhouette composition.
  3. Swap the subject for 3 more: a pumpkin king, a haunted mansion, a black cat familiar — one pack, four posts.
  4. Post one portrait per day in the week before Halloween with a one-line spooky caption.
  5. Ask a question in the caption — 'which one is watching you?' — to trigger comments.

💡 Pro Tips

  • Portrait-orientation images take more feed space on mobile and earn more shares than landscapes.
  • Watermark subtly in a corner; viral Halloween images get reposted without credit constantly.
